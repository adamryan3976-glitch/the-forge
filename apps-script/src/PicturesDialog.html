Every picture saves as soon as you paste it, so there's nothing else to save.

1. Import from a Google Form

Paste one Form's edit link (do one Form per grade). Picture blocks in the Form are saved to a private Math Check-In Pictures folder in your Drive, and the Form's answer key is checked against the app's.

2. Paste the rest

For pictures attached to a question: in the Form, right-click the picture → Copy image, then click a box below and press Ctrl+V. You can also drop in an image file or a screenshot.
